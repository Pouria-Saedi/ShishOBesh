The Exterior

Persepolis, carved in walnut.

At the crown of the design sits Persepolis's own fortress brickwork, and below it the Faravahar, cut with careful line-work and real depth of relief. Tall Achaemenid column capitals frame the scene on either side.

Underneath: soldiers of the Immortal Guard, water-lily flowers, a lamassu — the winged bull — and a winged lion, each carved with the same unhurried patience as the rest of the case.

No two boards read quite the same. The interior is cut from natural walnut root — the grain decides the pattern before we ever touch a blade to the case around it.

Built to Play On

Made for daily use, not just display.

Hidden Hinges

The hinges sit flush and hidden within the case, so it opens and closes on a clean, uninterrupted line — nothing to catch a sleeve or snag a cloth.

Sloped Rails

The rail edges are sloped, so a die that hits them rolls back onto the surface instead of stopping dead — noticeable every time you actually play.

Hardwax Finish

A hardwax coating seals the case against scratches and moisture, so it can be handled and played on for years without babying it.

Living With It

Simple care, long life.

Keep it out of direct, intense sunlight. Wipe clean with a soft, dry cloth, and keep it away from water and sharp objects. Try not to leave it half-open for long stretches, and store it somewhere dry and cool when it's not on the table.
